2027 holiday calendar

A 2027 calendar with the public holidays of the country you choose, worked out from each country’s own rules: Easter-linked dates, Monday substitutes when a holiday falls on a weekend, and observed days. 42 countries, including the US, the UK, Canada, Australia and most of Europe.

What you can change

  • 42 countries, each from its own rules rather than a copied list
  • Weekend substitutes: in 2027 the US observes Independence Day on Monday 5 July and Christmas on Friday 24 December
  • UK bank holidays for England and Wales, including Easter Monday on 29 March 2027
  • Several countries at once, for families and teams split across borders

Printing it correctly

Choose Print, then pick Save as PDF as the destination if you want a file. Set scale to 100% and margins to None — the sheet already carries its exact paper size, so the browser must not rescale it. Anything else and the dimensions you chose are no longer the dimensions on the page.

Questions

Which countries are covered?

Argentina, Australia, Austria, Belgium, Brazil, Bulgaria, Canada, Chile, Croatia, the Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, India, Ireland, Italy, Japan, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Mexico, the Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Russia, Slovakia, Slovenia, South Africa, South Korea, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, the United Kingdom and the United States.

Does it include regional holidays?

No. It shows national public holidays. For the UK that means the England and Wales bank holidays.
