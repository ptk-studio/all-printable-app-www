Printable bingo cards

Unique bingo cards in one pass, with numbers drawn per column the way B-I-N-G-O does — 1 to 15 under B, 61 to 75 under O — or your own words in the squares.

Open the maker Free · no sign-up · nothing uploaded

What you can change

  • Numbers with proper per-column ranges, or your own word list
  • Grids from 3 × 3 to 6 × 6, with an optional free centre
  • Header letters you can change or remove
  • Up to 40 cards, two or four to a sheet

Printing it correctly

Choose Print, then pick Save as PDF as the destination if you want a file. Set scale to 100% and margins to None — the sheet already carries its exact paper size, so the browser must not rescale it. Anything else and the dimensions you chose are no longer the dimensions on the page.

Questions

Are the cards different from each other?

Yes — each is dealt separately from the sheet’s seed.

Can I use words instead of numbers?

Yes. Give it at least as many words as there are squares.
