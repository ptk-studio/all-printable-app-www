Is the paper really to scale?

Yes, and it is measured rather than assumed. Each sheet carries its exact media size, and the grid generator is checked against a rendered print: a 10 mm pitch comes out at 10 mm, a 0.3 mm rule at 0.3 mm.

How do I get a PDF?

Print, then choose “Save as PDF” as the destination. Set scale to 100% and margins to None — the page already carries the exact paper size, so the browser does not need to guess.

Is anything uploaded?

No. There is no server-side rendering and no analytics on the makers. A photo you add to a calendar is read locally and never leaves the browser.

Can I come back to a design?

Yes. Settings are stored in the page address, so bookmarking the link restores exactly what you had. You can also save named setups in the sidebar.

Which years work?

Any of them. Holidays are computed from each country’s rules — including Easter-linked dates and weekend substitutions — rather than looked up in a table that goes stale.
