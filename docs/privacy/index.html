Privacy

Last updated: November 17, 2026. This page covers the All Printable makers at app.all-printable.com. The home page at all-printable.com has its own short privacy page.

Your printables stay in your browser

The calendars, planners, papers and other printables are made in your browser. What you type into them (event names, habits, addresses, word lists and so on) is not sent to us, unless you choose to save a design to your account (below).

Usage statistics, only if you agree

We use Google Analytics (through Firebase) only if you opt in. Until you agree, it isn't loaded and no analytics cookie is set. If you agree, we record interface choices only, such as which maker, layout or paper size you used, never the content of your printables. You can change your choice at any time from the link in the page footer; it is remembered in your browser.

Signing in (optional)

You can use every free maker without an account. If you sign in, you do it with Google, through Firebase Authentication, which keeps you signed in using your browser's storage. We then store a small account record in Google Cloud Firestore: your account id, your email address, when the account was created, whether you have Pro, and your Pro settings (such as a custom sheet footer). Designs you choose to save to your account are stored there too, so you can open them on another device. Only you can read your account record and saved designs. You can delete a saved design at any time, and you can ask us to delete your account at the address below.

Buying Pro

Pro is billed by Stripe. Payment details go directly to Stripe and never reach us. We receive the subscription status and Stripe's customer and subscription ids, linked to your account, so we can turn Pro on and off.

Feedback

If you send us feedback in All Printable (through the feedback button, form or app screen), we receive your message, the kind of feedback you chose, an optional star rating, the page or app version you were using, and your language setting. We ask for your email only if you want a reply, and we keep it only if you tick the box allowing us to contact you; we delete it after at most 90 days. We don't use cookies or tracking for feedback, and we don't collect device identifiers. We also store the IP address your feedback was sent from, to prevent abuse; it is deleted with your feedback after one year. Feedback is stored by PTK Studio's 88eggs service (hosted in the United States), used only to fix problems and improve All Printable, never sold or shared, and deleted after one year. To have your feedback deleted sooner, contact us at hello@ptk-studio.com.

Contact

All Printable is made by PTK Studio. Questions about privacy, or to delete your account: hello@ptk-studio.com.
