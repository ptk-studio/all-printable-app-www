## 2. Direction for 2027

- **Who it's for:**
  1. **Planners and home organizers** who search for a calendar, planner or tracker for a specific year, month or country, and want to change it before printing.
  2. **Teachers and homeschool parents** who need maths drills, graph paper, handwriting sheets, word searches and bingo with answer keys, at true scale.
  3. **Families and kids** (revision, Owner (e)): people who'd rather print a calendar, chore chart, puzzle or colouring page at home than buy a book. Each of the three audiences (families, teachers, kids) gets its own landing hub and presets (B7).
- **Two ways to sell one engine** (revision, Owner (a)): the free maker on the web, and **print-ready KDP interiors** from the same engines (sudoku, word search, mazes, later crosswords and planners), sold on HoneyStorm's shelf (B5). A person who'd rather not buy the book can always print the same kind of sheet free.
- **What it is:** the **free, no-ads, no-signup printable maker**. Every sheet can be customized in the browser, prints at real size, and stays on your machine. Pro ($5/month) removes the credit line and unlocks extra presets. It never takes away what's free.
- **What it isn't:**
  - a design suite like Canva;
  - a template marketplace like Etsy or Template.net;
  - an ad-funded PDF library;
  - an email-gated "freebie" site.
- **How it fits PTK Studio:**
  - the portfolio's only web subscription product and its SEO laboratory;
  - the engine behind free printables for HoneyStorm (puzzle samples) and Otto & Lotti (colouring pages);
  - cross-links from both of those.

**Trends**
- **Two calendar peaks a year:** January (new year) and August (school year), with December as the run-up (RankHero, above). The year pages must be live by **August** for the school year and by **October** for the next calendar year.
- **The free tier is ad-funded:** Calendarpedia, Printable Paradise and CalendarLabs all run AdSense. timeanddate sells "ad-free plus logo removal" for **$14.99/year**, the closest precedent to our Pro.
- **Search clicks are shrinking for informational queries:** AI Overviews on ~20% of results, with large CTR drops. "Printable" queries are a want-to-make-something intent that a summary can't print, but they're **not proven immune** (no study found for "printable" queries).
- **AI-made printables are contested on Etsy:** new AI rules (reported October 2026; banned patterns and recipes; disclosure for digital downloads), and buyers complain of undisclosed AI ([Value Added Resource, accessed 2026-11-15](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-bans-ai-patterns/); vendor blogs differ, **unverified** against Etsy's own page).

## Quality, delight and satisfaction

**Top pain points**
- Ours:
  - nobody finds the site;
  - the Pro sign-in wall (sessions don't carry over from all-printable.com; `METRICS.md`);
  - a sticky header and tagline layout issue (#51);
  - internal check gaps (#45, #47, #49, #50).
- Competitors': billing surprises, paywalls, lost work, bot-only support, ads and email gates (above).

**Delight improvements**
1. **A "print a test strip" ruler** on paper and grid sheets that proves true scale, with a 10 cm line.
2. **One-click "next year"** on every calendar and planner, so last year's design reopens for the new year from the saved link.
3. **School-year presets** (Aug–Jul) with local term holidays where we have the data.
4. **Faster first print:** each landing page opens the maker already set to that page's sheet (the deep links from #60), aiming for print in under 30 seconds.
5. **Accessibility:** large-print and high-contrast options on worksheets and planners, and keyboard-operable controls.

**Quality bar**
- Every merge passes the release checks (smoke-live 6/6, HEALTH-CHECK 6/6, `check-site-urls`, generator drift, secret scan; RELEASE-CHECKS.md).
- Printed output matches the stated size within 1 mm on Letter and A4.
- Holidays spot-checked for US and UK each year before the season page goes live.
- Lighthouse performance ≥ 90 on landing pages (to measure in Q1).
- 0 failed `checkout_error` events a month once there's traffic.

**How satisfaction is measured (every 2-week review)**
- Return visits (GA4 Day 1 retention, once there's volume).
- `print`, `copy_link` and `download_html` events per maker session.
- Feedback-form sentiment (FEEDBACK.md, #73).
- Pro churn and `billing_portal` opens (intent, not cancellations).
- Search Console CTR for the season pages.
